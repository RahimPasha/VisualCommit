| D47 | 2026-10-09 | Commits are loaded by one `git log --date-order` over `--branches --remotes --tags` and `HEAD`, read line by line as git prints them, and handed to the graph in pages: the first 100 commits as soon as they are read, then pages of up to 2,000.
